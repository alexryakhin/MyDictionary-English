Questions

Is My Dictionary free?

Yes. Saving words, flashcards, word games, progress and iCloud sync are free. AI features — definitions, AI quizzes, photo scans and Agent Pip — run on AI credits: 100 to start and a top-up to 50 every month. Pro gives you unlimited AI.

Which languages can I learn?

Any language you're learning — more than 135 are supported for definitions and pronunciation. The app itself is available in 34 languages, and you choose which language the definitions are written in.

Does it sync between my devices?

Yes. Your dictionary syncs through your own iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac. You can also share a dictionary with friends or a study group.

What happens to my data?

Your words and progress stay on your devices and in your iCloud. There are no ads, and we never sell your data. We only collect anonymous usage analytics and what's needed to validate a subscription. Privacy Policy

What is Agent Pip?

Your AI tutor inside the app. Ask about your words, get an explanation when something doesn't click, and add new vocabulary straight from the chat.
